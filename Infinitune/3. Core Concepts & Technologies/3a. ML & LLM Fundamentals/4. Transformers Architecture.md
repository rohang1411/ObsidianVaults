

## 1. Simple Explanation (Analogy)

The Transformer is the model architecture (the "blueprint") that makes modern [[1. Large Language Models (LLMs)]] possible. Its key invention is [[3. Self-Attention Mechanisms|Self-Attention]].

**Analogy: The Reading Group**

* **Old Way (RNNs):** Imagine trying to understand a long sentence by reading it one word at a time, trying to remember everything that came before. "The... cat... who... sat... on... the... mat... was...". By the time you get to "was," you might have forgotten the "cat." This is how older models (RNNs) worked.

* **The Transformer Way (Self-Attention):** Now, imagine a reading group. Everyone gets the *full sentence* at the same time: "The cat who sat on the mat was orange."
    * To understand the word "was," the other words in the group "look" at each other.
    * The word "cat" shouts, "I'm the subject, 'was' is related to me!"
    * The word "mat" shouts, "I'm not as important to 'was'."
    * The Transformer architecture allows every word to look at every other word in the sentence simultaneously and decide *how much attention* to pay to each one. This gives it a much deeper and more immediate understanding of context.

## 2. Why It Was Used in InfiniTune

The InfiniTune report explicitly states that "a key component of modern LLMs is the use of Transformer architecture". The base model, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, is a Transformer-based model.

The project doesn't *change* the Transformer architecture itself. Instead, it targets this architecture for updates using [[3. LoRA (Low-Rank Adaptation)|LoRA]], which works by injecting small, trainable adapter layers *into* the existing Transformer layers.

## 3. Pros & Cons

| Pros | Cons |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Parallelization:** Unlike RNNs which are sequential (word 1, then word 2...), Transformers process all words at once. This makes them *much faster* to train on modern parallel hardware like GPUs. | **Computationally Intensive:** The [[Self-Attention_Mechanisms|Self-Attention]] mechanism is very effective but computationally costly. |
| **Handles Long-Range Dependencies:** Excellent at understanding how a word at the beginning of a paragraph relates to a word at the end. | **High Memory Usage:** The attention calculations for very long sentences can consume a lot of GPU memory. |
| **The Standard:** It is the state-of-the-art and foundational architecture for virtually all top-performing LLMs (GPT, BERT, Qwen). | **Complex:** The architecture is more complex than older models, with multiple heads, layers, and skip connections. |
